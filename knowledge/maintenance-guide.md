# Project maintenance review guide
Source: project-authored demonstration guidance. This is not a manufacturer manual.

## Failure risk review
A failure alert means the classifier estimates that an engine is within its configured
failure horizon. Review sensor data quality, the alert threshold, and independent
anomaly evidence before prioritizing engineering inspection. A low estimated failure
probability does not guarantee safe operation.

## Anomaly investigation
An anomaly indicates that sensor window features differ from healthy training observations.
Inspect missing readings, duplicate cycles, abrupt changes, and operating settings.
Anomalies may reflect sensor issues or operating regimes rather than an impending failure.

## Sensor trends and historical comparison
Sensor trends and correlations are observational. Operating conditions can explain
changes in temperature, pressure, speed, and flow measurements. Similar historical
machines provide context, but their observed remaining life is not a new RUL prediction.

## Documentation and recommendations
Use equipment-specific approved maintenance manuals and qualified engineering review
for physical maintenance procedures. This demonstration guide does not specify repairs,
replacement intervals, torque settings, or permission to operate equipment.
